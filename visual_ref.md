# Central Warsaw visual reference (as of Oct 2026), for a 1 m voxel model

Confidence: **[P]** = I saw it in a dated 2025–26 photo. **[T]** = it is stated in an article or official text. **[E]** = my own estimate or general knowledge, so treat it as uncertain.
Hex codes are approximate daylight values sampled by eye from the photos.

## 1. "Zielona Złota" (ul. Złota, Marszałkowska → Zgoda) + the surrounding quarter

**Status and design**
- Opened to the public on **3 Aug 2026** (Złota and Zgoda) [T].
- Landscape design by RS Architektura Krajobrazu (Dorota Rudawa). Built by Skanska. Cost 49.1 mln zł. Area ~2.5 ha [T].
- The street is a woonerf. There is **no asphalt carriageway left and no kerbed roadway**. Cars (residents, deliveries) share the light granite lanes, and retractable bollards control access [T][P].

**Cross-section, from facade to facade** [P]. Widths are estimates from photos, with the total street ~22–25 m [E].
- **Each side: a light granite lane along the buildings, ~4–5 m wide.**
  - Large rectangular slabs in light grey granite, **#C9C8C3** (wet: #A9A9A6), laid in a running bond along the street.
  - On the green side of the lane runs a ~0.5 m band of textured/tactile granite setts, **#B8B7B2**.
- **Building-side tree row, one on each side.** Young trees, ~6–9 m tall, set ~6–8 m apart. They stand in strips of shrubs and perennials beside the facades. Some low rope "fences" on short dark posts.
- **Central zone, ~10–12 m wide: a "park" strip (there is no single straight central alley).** It is made of:
  - **Mineral (decomposed granite) surface**, beige/sand **#D8CCB5**, as irregular meandering paths and small plazas.
  - **Planting islands** of perennials, mostly lavender/perovskia purple **#8E86B8** plus green **#4F6B3A**. Their edges are formed by **light concrete/stone bench-kerbs** (#D9D6CF) with angled wedge-shaped seat blocks.
  - **Lawn patches** (#7E8F4A in late summer, partly brown).
  - **Additional trees scattered in the islands.** The overall impression is about 2 loose rows of trees inside the central strip plus 1 row at each side, i.e. ~4 lines in total. Exact positions are uncertain.
  - City text: "central lawn, light alleys, **two rows of trees along each side**". News text: "central path with mineral surface, **81 elms**" [T].
- Benches: some have wooden slat seats (#9A6A3E) on stone bases, plus the concrete edge seats. Some benches are lit from underneath at night [P].
- Lamps: slim **dark anthracite poles, #3A3F44**, ~5–6 m tall, with lantern/bell heads [P].

**Tree count** [T]
- 81 elms on Złota. More than 100 trees on Złota and Zgoda together. ~167–200 trees across the whole quarter. They were planted 9 m tall in spring 2026.

**Fountain 2: a "dry" plaza fountain at the former tunnel ramp next to Marszałkowska** [P][T]
- The main, larger fountain. It is a flat, walk-on plaza of **dark speckled grey granite, #55595C**, cut into **irregular polygonal slabs** with even darker shard-shaped inlays (#3E4144).
- It is set flush into the surrounding light granite and edged by slot drains.
- Size: about **12–14 m wide (most of the street width) × ~25–30 m long** along the street axis [E].
- **About 50–80 jets** come straight out of the paving through stainless rings, on a roughly **2 m grid** [P]. It is lit at night.
- The former tunnel ramps covered ~1,500 m². Underneath is a rainwater retention tank; the two tanks under Złota together hold 432 m³ [T].

**Fountain 1: a "mountain" mist fountain near Złota 7/8, mid-block** [P][T]
- An oval lawn ~12 × 8 m holding **~22 rough cream sandstone boulders, #D6CFC0**, each ~1–2 m and ~0.6–0.9 m high.
- Irregular flagstone stepping-paths (#C9C3B6) lead in, to a small central dark grey flagstone pad with mist and jet nozzles.

**Dom pod Orłami square (Jasna/Zgoda)**
- Finished by mid-August 2026 [P].
- Light granite paving, angled planted beds, wooden benches, young trees.
- The facade behind it is ochre-yellow sandstone, **#C9A86A**.

**Still under construction in autumn 2026** [T]
- **Sienkiewicza (Marszałkowska–Jasna):** closed to traffic since 15 Apr 2026. On 27 Aug 2026 paving was still being laid. Plans: a tree row along the carriageway, green squares replacing parking, and a circular mist pergola at Zgoda 12.
- **Jasna:** work ongoing.
- **Pl. Emila Młynarskiego / "Ogród Muzyki"** (former parking in front of the Filharmonia): only the paving had started in August.
- The official end date is "autumn 2026". No completion announcement had appeared by early September.
- **For an Oct 2026 snapshot: model Sienkiewicza and Młynarskiego as building sites or with partly laid paving [E].**

**Crossings at Marszałkowska** [T]
- At-grade zebra on the Złota axis (opened 5 Nov 2025).
- At-grade zebra on the Sienkiewicza/Fangora axis.

## 2. Plac Centralny im. T. Mazowieckiego (opened 4 Jun 2025)

**Basics** [T]
- Design by A-A Collective. Area **>23,000 m²**, of which **>3,300 m² is lawn**.
- **109 trees**: robinia, ash, gleditsia, Kentucky coffeetree, ginkgo, magnolia, dogwood.
- **Six stone types** (one source says five).

**Extent** [P, July 2025 aerial]
- The finished part is the band in front of the PKiN east facade and around MSN / the Świętokrzyska end.
- **The southern half, toward Złota and the Rotunda, was still an asphalt parking lot / bus area in mid-2025.**
- Check whether this changed by 2026. I found nothing saying it did [E].

**Paving logic: a 1939 map drawn in stone** [T][P]
- **Former street lines (Wielka, Zielna, Złota, Chmielna, etc.)** are strips of grey granite setts, mixed **#8E8D89 / #A09E98**.
- **Former courtyards** use granite setts reclaimed from Plac Defilad (same grey), mixed with granite slabs taken from around the old tribune.
- **Footprints of the pre-war tenements** are large-format slabs in **grey (#B5B4AF)** and **red/red-brown (#9A5E4E)**. In many places the footprints are instead **lawns** (#6E9A3E): rectangular, sharp-edged, with grey stepping slabs.
- Main walking surfaces are light grey granite slabs, **#C4C3BE**.
- Plaques/inlays such as "Tu była ulica Wielka" mark the old streets.

**Water** [T][P]
- A low, shallow **pond (sadzawka)**, rectangular-ish (its outline is a former courtyard), roughly ~25 × 10 m [E].
- It sits **in front of the PKiN main east portico**, edged with grey setts, ~5–10 cm of water acting as a mirror.
- There is no fountain. The originally planned large basin at the Marszałkowska entrance was dropped.

**Furniture** [P]
- Wooden-slat benches and chairs (#A06A3A) on **dark grey/black steel frames**. Square multi-person benches. Semicircular seats around trees.
- Tall dark lamp poles with double heads.

**Old honorary tribune**: grey granite, **#9C9A94**. It was rebuilt closer to Marszałkowska and is partly overgrown [T].

**Plaza next to MSN**: light grey granite slabs, plus wide grey stone steps and a parapet in front of MSN [P].

**North of MSN** [P]
- The new TR Warszawa theatre building was under construction (crane) in June 2026.

## 3. PKiN (Palace of Culture and Science)

**Facade** [P]
- Ceramic/terracotta-type cladding blocks imitating sandstone.
- Sunlit colour: warm beige-ochre **#C8B48F**. In shade or weathered: grey-beige **#9E9482**. Darker streaks in the recesses.
- The attics with pinnacles/parapet "crowns" and the corner obelisks are **the same sandstone colour**, not a separate colour.
- Main windows: dark, #2E3338.

**Roofs** [P][E]
- Mostly **flat, grey bitumen/membrane (#6F6F6C)**, hidden behind the attics.
- Sala Kongresowa (west rotunda): a flat grey dome.

**Top of the tower** [P, 23 Sep 2026 close-up]
- **Clock storey**: sandstone colour. **4 clock faces, one per side, each 6 m across**, on the 40th floor.
  - **White/off-white dial (#F2F0EA)**, black Roman numerals, **black hands** (hour 2.7 m, minute 3.54 m), dark bronze rim.
  - Lit at night (they read clearly in dusk photos).
- **Above the clock**: an arcaded lantern storey, then a **pyramidal/ogee cap in verdigris copper green (#4E7F6A)**.
- **Then a dark bronze/blackish sphere (#3A352C)**.
- **Then the spire: gilded/brass lattice, #B49C3C** (it reads gold), with a small finial. Total height 237 m.

**Night lighting** [P][E]
- Floodlit facade plus LED colour on the tower, a colour system in place since ~2010.
- Recent photos show:
  - Sep 2026: **warm golden-white over the whole building (#F2C97A)**.
  - Aug 2025: the tower in **lilac/violet-white (#C9B3F0)** with a warm-lit base.
  - The spire is lit gold.
- Themed colours appear on occasion, such as the white-red flag colours.
- **Suggested default: warm white/gold. Uncertain: I found no official statement of the default scheme.**

## 4. Other landmarks

**Varso Tower (310 m)** [P][E]
- Glass curtain wall, **neutral blue-grey reflective (#8DA0B2)**, with a slim light grid.
- The **spire is a white/light-grey steel lattice mast (#E6E8EA)** carrying **3–4 round white disc platforms** and a thin top needle (photo 23 Sep 2026).
- The lower Varso 1/2 blocks are darker glass (#3A4650) with black frames. There is a white-clad mid-rise block (#E9E6DF) at the Chmielna base.

**Złota 44** [P][T]
- Façade is about 60 % aluminium cassettes and 40 % glass.
- It reads as a **silver-white (#D9DCDA) and green-tinted glass (#6E8C86) / dark reflective (#3D4A4E) mosaic** in irregular vertical stripes.
- **Crown**: a flat roof block on the street side (plant on the roof) plus the **curved "wing/sail" blade on the opposite side**. The blade is clad in white bands and rises ~15–20 m above the roof to a sharp point. Height 192 m.

**MSN (Museum of Modern Art)**
- A pure **white box, #F1F0EC**, with smooth panels and deep-cut, recessed window openings [P].
- A colonnade at ground level. Flat white roof with light panels.
- Lit with cool white at night.

**Warszawa Centralna** [P]
- Low glazed hall with a cantilevered roof of **hyperbolic-paraboloid shells**.
- Roof edges and fascias are **light grey-white concrete/metal (#D2D3D0)**, with mid-grey undersides (#9A9B98).
- Walls: **dark grey-tinted glass (#3B4650)** with grey mullions. Grey concrete columns.

**Złote Tarasy** [P, Sep 2026 aerial]
- An **undulating, "bubbly" glass roof** of rhombic/triangular panes, reflective **blue-grey/silver (#8FA2B4)** with darker patches (#4E5D6C).
- The Multikino block next to it has a **pale mint-green metal roof (#A6CDBB)** and a large green roof (#6C8E45).
- A round white skylight drum sits by the entrance.

**Rotunda PKO** [P]
- A cylinder of **green-tinted glass (#3F6E66)**.
- **Radial folded ("fan") roof in silver-grey metal (#C4C7CA)** with a zig-zag eave and a round central skylight.

## 5. Street furniture and transport
- **Lamp poles, Marszałkowska and the main roads**: tall (~10–12 m) **anthracite/graphite steel (#3A3E42)**, usually with twin or bracketed LED heads [P]. Złota uses the same dark colour on lower lantern poles.
- **Bike paths**: **red asphalt, #A8453E**. Seen on Emilii Plater, Świętokrzyska and Marszałkowska in the aerials [P].
- **Pavements on main streets**: grey square concrete or granite slabs (#A9A8A3). Tactile strips are grey [P].
- **Trams**:
  - Warsaw livery is **yellow (#F5C200) body + red (#C8102E) roof band/upper edge + black window band**.
  - New Hyundai Rotem 140N trams (~33 m, 5 sections) are increasingly common, alongside Pesa Jazz/Swing in the same colours [P].
  - Track bed: green grass track in many places (Marszałkowska was renewed in 2025) [P].
- **Buses (MZA)**: **yellow body (#F2C200) with a red band/skirt (#C8102E)** [P].
- **Bus/tram shelters**: silver-grey/graphite metal frame with glass walls and a glass roof, ~1–1.5 m deep × 4–8 m long. Many have city-information or advertising panels [P/E]. I found no official colour spec.
- **Bollards**: short black/anthracite steel posts [P].
