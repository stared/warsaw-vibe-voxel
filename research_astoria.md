# Astoria Premium Offices: model notes

Address: ul. Przeskok 2. In 2023 the street was renamed **ul. Kazimierza Brokla**, and street signs now say Brokla. OSM way 306124863, Wikidata Q107862777.
Photos are in `scratchpad/astoria/`: front.jpg, west.jpg, north.jpg (Strabag), commons_2021.jpg, commons_brokla_zgoda.jpg, commons_pkin_view.jpg (aerial from PKiN).

## Basics
- **Architect:** Epstein (Warsaw), with concept design by Fiszer Atelier 41. The developer and general contractor was STRABAG. Sources: urbanity.pl/mazowieckie/warszawa/budynek-przy-ul-przeskok,b8495 and propertydesign.pl (…biurowiec_astoria_projektu_epstein…,3002.html). The guess "Kuryłowicz" is wrong.
- **Dates:** Built 2013/14 to July 2016, opened December 2016. LEED Platinum. Source: urbanity, strabag-real-estate.com/en/project/astoria-premium-offices-warschau/.
- **Height:** **45 m**. There are **11 storeys above ground** and 2 below. Sources: urbanity, Wikidata. Strabag says "10 floors" and Colliers says "12-storey". Use 11.
- **Massing:** The 11 storeys stand on the Zgoda / PKiN-axis side, topped by a drum ("glorieta"). The Szpitalna side has **8 storeys**. Source: wot.waw.pl/en/warszawa-przyszlosci/astoria-premium-offices/. north.jpg shows the east part about 9–10 storeys high at the street line, with 1–2 glazed stepped-back top floors (setback about 2–3 m). The exact steps are UNCERTAIN.
- **Roof:** Flat. OSM has roof:colour=lightgreen, and the PKiN aerial shows a green/sedum roof (#7f8f5a) with plant boxes. Strabag mentions "2 terraces", likely on the setbacks; their positions are uncertain.

## Plan (from OSM, local metres, origin 52.2330 N / 21.0137 E, x=E, y=N)
- The footprint is an elongated wedge about **100 m long (W–E) × 25–40 m deep**. The **west tip is a full semicircle about 12–14 m in diameter**, centred near (-42,14) and facing the Brokla × Zgoda × Złota junction and the PKiN axis.
- **Fronts:**
  - The NNW side (~75 m), from (-24,27) to (42,49), faces **Brokla (ex-Przeskok)**, across the street from the stone WSA courthouse (Jasna 2/4).
  - The SW side, from the tip to about (0,2), faces **Zgoda**.
  - The short east end, from (42,49) to (53,28), faces the **Szpitalna** side. wot.waw.pl says the site lies between Przeskok, Zgoda and Szpitalna.
  - The SE side, from (53,28) via (35,22) to (-1,10), is a party wall / courtyard edge against Zgoda 6.
- **Corners:** The west tip is round. The NE corner is a sharp (square) corner. There is no atrium, only a light-well/courtyard edge on the SE side (uncertain).

## Facade
There are two characters (sources: sztuka-architektury.pl/article/4298, wot.waw.pl).
- **West half + round tower ("glass"):** Almost fully glazed curtain wall. Horizontal **dark bronze/black spandrel bands** at every floor (#2b2a28, ~0.4 m tall) are curved around the tip. Slim vertical **champagne/bronze mullion caps** (#b49a6c) sit every ~1.35–1.5 m (module UNCERTAIN). The glass is neutral grey-blue and reflective (#4f6472 by day, #2e3a44 in shadow). Some bays have internal blinds (#5a5a58). The overall read is very dark, which matches OSM building:colour=black. Use a dark frame #262626 with glass #3e4f5c.
- **East half ("tenement"):** Light **travertine-texture architectural-concrete pilasters/panels** (~1300 m², Reckli travertine matrix; colour off-white/light warm grey #d9d5cc) set between deep-set **dark bronze window surrounds** (#33302d). The windows are tall and narrow, about 1.4 m wide, in a rhythm of 2 windows per pilaster bay (~3 m) with pilasters ~1 m wide. Sources: beton-architektoniczny.pl/realizacje/elewacje/13-astoria-premium-offices and north.jpg.
- **Horizontal bands:** Thin warm **LED cornice lines** (#ffb347 at night) run above the ground floor/mezzanine and above about the 6th floor on the east part. These are strong horizontal accents at night.
- **Floor heights:** Ground floor + mezzanine about 6 m. Typical office floor about 3.7–3.8 m (45 m / 11, estimated).

## Ground floor
- The ground floor is **set back/undercut and fully glazed**, behind a colonnade of dark square piers. It is not an arcade, just a slightly recessed shopfront line with dark (#1e1e1e) ribbed metal pier cladding. Source: sztuka-architektury.pl/article/3022 and the photos.
- The **round tip holds Green Caffè Nero** (gold "green CAFFÈ NERO" lettering and umbrellas outside). There are more shops along Brokla with thin canopies over the doors. Alior Bank is NOT verified.
- The shopfront glass reads warm-lit (#e8c48a interior glow) at night.

## Distinctive features
- **Crown drum** on the round tip: a dark bronze cylindrical band (#3a2f28), about 1 storey tall, set slightly proud. Backlit gold serif lettering **"ASTORIA Premium Offices"** (#d9b77a) runs round it; it later showed a leasing phone number. The top has a thin metal cap.
- **At night:** The vertical bronze mullion caps glow warm along the tower, and the LED cornice lines are lit.
- **Business Link Astoria coworking** is on floors 8–10 (not checked here).
