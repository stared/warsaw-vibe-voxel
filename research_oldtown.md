# Old Town, New Town, Plac Zamkowy, Praga bank: model notes

Scope: details LiDAR cannot give (colour, material, thin structures, facade features). Heights and roof shapes come from LiDAR. Where a number below disagrees with LiDAR, trust LiDAR, but check the photos first.
Confidence: **[T]** = stated in the cited source. **[E]** = my estimate or general knowledge; verify against photos. Hex values are always [E] (daylight, by eye).
Commons categories were checked on 2026-11-02 with the Commons API (prop=categoryinfo). File counts are direct files only; subcategories hold more. Commons names these places inconsistently, often after Wikidata P373, so the English Wikipedia title is often NOT the category name. Use the names below exactly.

## Plac Zamkowy and the castle

### Zamek Królewski (Plac Zamkowy 4)
- **Plan:** an irregular pentagon around an inner courtyard [T].
  - The **west (Sigismund) facade** faces Plac Zamkowy. It is about **90 m long** [T en.wiki].
  - The **Saxon facade** (Elewacja Saska) faces the Vistula, on top of the escarpment.
- **West facade:**
  - Plastered and painted a **brick-red / terracotta**, about **#B55A3F** [E].
  - Window surrounds, the rusticated corners, the gate portals and the cornice are light stone/stucco, about **#DCD2BC** [E].
  - Three storeys plus a roof storey, with a **corner turret at each end** of the west wing [T: "wieżyczki narożne"]. The turrets carry small domed copper caps [E].
- **Roofs:** copper sheet, weathered **dark brown to grey-green**, about **#55594C** [E]. It is NOT the bright green patina of Pałac pod Blachą. Check this in photos.
- **Wieża Zegarowa (Zygmuntowska):**
  - Stands in the middle of the west facade. It is **60 m** tall [T en.wiki].
  - The square shaft has **four clock faces**, installed in 1974 [T]. The dials are dark with gilded hands and numerals [E].
  - On top is a tall baroque helmet in several tiers with an open lantern, a gilded ball and a spire, in dark copper [E].
  - The bugle call (hejnał) is played from the tower at 11:15 [T].
- **Wieża Władysławowska:** a staircase tower that stands **on the inner courtyard, east side**, not on the square. Commons photos are captioned "Innenhof, Ostseite" and "Inner courtyard … including Władysław's Tower". It has a copper cupola [E].
- **Wieża Grodzka:** the old Gothic "Great Tower", at the south-east, next to Pałac pod Blachą [T]. Its lower part is medieval red brick [T, survived 1944].
- **Saxon (Vistula) facade:** the original colour was restored in 2012–13 [T]. Take the colour from photos; I recall a light ochre/cream, about #E3D3A8 [E].
- **Arkady Kubickiego** (1818–21):
  - A classicist arcade substructure under the Saxon facade [T].
  - **195 m long and 9.5 m deep**, with an 8.5 m vault span. The terrace on top is **195 × 24 m** [T fotopolska.eu, National Geographic PL].
  - A single-storey arcade of round arches facing the gardens, plastered pale ochre/cream, about #E2D2A6 [E].
  - Restored 1996–2009 (S. Fiszer). It links the middle and lower castle gardens [T].

### Pałac pod Blachą (Plac Zamkowy 2)
- A late-baroque palace at the foot of the castle, south of it, on the slope toward the river [T].
- It is named after its **copper-sheet roof**, which was unusual at the time [T]. Before that it had tiles, as Bellotto painted it [T]. The high roof was restored in 1932–37 [T].
- **Colours:** the roof is weathered copper, greener than the castle's, about **#6F8F78** [E]. The walls are pale ochre/yellow with white trim, about #E6CF96 [E, verify]. It has a U-plan with side wings [T].
- **Sources:** https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pa%C5%82ac_Pod_Blach%C4%85
- **Commons:** `Palace under the sheet metal roof` (86)

### Kolumna Zygmunta III Wazy
- **Total height: 22 m** [T pl.wiki]. **Statue: 2.75 m**, bronze [T].
  - The king wears armour, a coronation cloak and a crown. He holds a sabre in his right hand and a cross in his left. His right foot rests on a plumed helmet [T].
  - The bronze is dark brown-green, about #4A4A3C [E]. The crown, cross and sabre are partly gilded [E].
- **Shaft:**
  - The current shaft (since 1949) is **Strzegom granite**, grey, about **#8C8A85** [T material, E colour].
  - The original shafts were red Chęciny conglomerate/"marble" (1644–1887) and granite (1887–1944). Both now lie on the ground by the castle's south wall [T].
- **Column details:**
  - A Corinthian **capital and base in bronze**, dark with gilt accents [E].
  - On the column socle stand **four bronze eagles** [T en.wiki].
  - The pedestal is stone with bronze plaques [T/E].
- **Base:** extra granite steps were added after the square was lowered by 0.5–1.5 m [T]. The base is roughly octagonal, with steps around a fountain basin [E, check ortho].
- **Position:** the column was moved **6 m north-east** and turned slightly in 1949 for the W-Z route [T].
- **Sources:**
  - https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolumna_Zygmunta_III_Wazy
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigismund%27s_Column
- **Commons:** `Sigismund's Column` (188). Its subcategories are `General views of the Sigismund Column` and `Details of the Sigismund Column`.

### Plac Zamkowy (surface)
- **Paving:** thick granite setts brought from Plac Grzybowski [T]. The mix is grey with pink-grey stones, about **#8E8782** [E].
- **Clinker-brick strips** across the square mark the former **double line of the city walls** [T]. They are dark red-brown, about #6E3A2C [E].
- **Most Gotycki:** at the north-east edge, by the Kamienica Johna side, a brick **bridge on two pointed-arch pier arcades** crosses the excavated moat [T].
- **The square:** a sloping triangle, Castle → Krakowskie Przedmieście. Its level was lowered 0.5–1.5 m post-war [T].
- **Escalators:** the escalator hall of the W-Z route sits in Kamienica Johna, on the north-east corner toward the Vistula [T].
- **Sources:** https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plac_Zamkowy_w_Warszawie

### Trasa W-Z tunnel and the escarpment
- **Tunnel:** a road + tram tunnel, **196 m long** [T]. It runs under Krakowskie Przedmieście, Senatorska and Miodowa, i.e. under the south end of Plac Zamkowy [T]. It was built by cut-and-cover in 1948, and the houses above were rebuilt [T].
- **Portals:**
  - The **west portal** opens onto al. Solidarności at Senatorska / Miodowa, west of the square [T: Świerczewski monument by the west exit, 1957].
  - The **east portal** is at the foot of the escarpment by St Anne's, above Mariensztat. Al. Solidarności then continues on a viaduct (the former Pancer viaduct line) down to Wybrzeże Kościuszkowskie and the bridge [T/E].
  - The portal fronts are clad in **sandstone**, cleaned in 1992–93 [T], about #CFC3A6 [E]. The east front has patinated bronze plaques [T].
- **Escalators:** 30 m long with a **12 m vertical rise** [T, 1949 originals; the replacements are 28 m]. That is a good check on the height of the square above the tunnel roadway.
- **Escarpment drop:**
  - About **18–20 m at the Royal Castle** and about **10 m at the New Town** [T].
  - About **25 m** from St Anne's south to the Belweder [T].
  - The W-Z tunnel cutting is about 10 m deep [T].
- **Sources:**
  - https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunel_Trasy_W-Z_w_Warszawie
  - https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trasa_W-Z_w_Warszawie
  - https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skarpa_warszawska
- **Commons:**

  - `Aleja Solidarności in Warsaw` (215)

## Old Town (Stare Miasto)

### Archikatedra św. Jana Chrzciciela (Świętojańska 8)
- A Vistula-Gothic brick hall church.
- **West front:** rebuilt 1948–56 (Zachwatowicz) as a **triangular, stepped, pinnacled Gothic gable**. It follows a Vogel watercolour and models such as Chełmno [T]. It has no tower; the original west tower fell in 1602 [T].
- **Walls:** **red brick**, about **#9A4632** [E]. The gable carries **white plastered blind panels** in pointed niches, about #EDE8DC [E]. There is a stone portal [E].
- **Roof:** **copper since about 1971**. From 1951 to about 1971 it was red monk-and-nun tile [T]. It is now weathered dark copper, brown-green [E].
- **Ganek:** the Royal Corridor, a covered bridge to the castle, crosses Dziekania [T: Commons category exists].
- **Sources:**

### Kościół Jezuitów / Sanktuarium MB Łaskawej (Świętojańska 10)
- Built 1609–26, Mannerist, directly north-east of the cathedral, wall to wall [T].
- The facade on Świętojańska is a **tall ornamental gable**, plastered light (cream/grey-beige, about #E2DACB [E]). There is a **dome over the apse** [T].
- Behind it, toward the escarpment, stands a **tall slender tower** with a cupola, which is prominent from the Vistula [E, verify on photos and LiDAR]. The roofs are copper [E].

### Rynek Starego Miasta
- **Size:** **90 × 73 m** [T en.wiki].
- **Houses:** about 3–5 storeys, with eaves at about 14–20 m [E, LiDAR]. Most are 3 window bays wide. They have steep gabled or ridge roofs in **red ceramic tile**, about **#9B4A34** [E]; some have attics or mansards.
- **Facades:** every house has a different plaster colour, many with **polychromy/sgraffito** and stone portals [T]. The original portals are at nos. 21 and 31, the polychromy at 20 and 40 [T].
  - Typical palette [E]: ochre #D9A441, terracotta #B55A3F, salmon #D98F6E, ivory #EFE3C4, sage #9DAA87, dusty blue-grey #8FA0A8, burgundy #7E3B3A.
  - Take each house from photos, using the per-house subcategories. The ground floors are often darker, with stone portals.
- **Strona Dekerta** (north, nos. 28–42):
  - Survived 1944 best (it had reinforced-concrete fire floors) and is the **Muzeum Warszawy** [T].
  - The 2016 renovation **restored the 1950s colours** [T].
  - Houses with own Commons categories: Baryczków (32), Pod Murzynkiem (36, a Black man's head on the facade), Kleinpoldowska, Falkiewicza, Kazubowska, Talenti (38), Gagatkiewicz (40), Montelupi (42).
- **Strona Barssa** (east, nos. 2–26, toward the escarpment):
  - The Literature Museum (Mickiewicz) is in it [T].
  - Houses with own Commons categories: Troperowska, Małodobry (12), Kupcewiczowska (14), Greyber (16), Orlemus (18), Burbachowska, Winklerowska, Pod Fortuną, Busserowska, Preys (26), Ellebrantowska.
- **Strona Kołłątaja** (west, nos. 15–31):
  - **Kamienica Fukierów (27)** is the wine house with a courtyard [T].
  - **Pod św. Anną (31)** has a Gothic portal [T].
  - Other houses: Simonetti, Markiewicz (17), Wójtowska (19), 21/21a, Urban (23), Rola (25), Gizów (29).
- **Strona Zakrzewskiego** (south, toward the castle, nos. 1–13):
  - Walbach (1), Jucht/"Pod Cyrulikiem" (3), **Pod Bazyliszkiem (5, basilisk sign)**, **Złocista (7)**, Długoszowa (9), Majeranowska (11) and **Pod Lwem (13, a painted lion on the facade)** [T names, E details].
- **Syrenka:**
  - A bronze by Konstanty Hegel (1855), standing **in the middle of a round fountain basin** [T].
  - The statue is about **2.5 m** tall [T Wikidata]. The base is granite/sandstone [T].
  - She raises a sword and holds a shield. The bronze is dark green-brown, about #4D5A4A [E].
  - The basin has a low dark iron railing [E]. The figure on the square is a copy; the original is in Muzeum Warszawy [E, about 2008].
  - It is placed off-centre in the square [E, check ortho].
- **Paving:** granite setts [E].

### Barbakan and the city walls
- **Barbakan:**
  - A **brick**, semicircular, two-storey gate bastion standing **in front of the moat** [T]. It was built in 1540 and rebuilt in 1952–54 [T]. Some of the bricks came from demolished buildings in Nysa and Wrocław [T].
  - It is crowned with a **Renaissance attic** (crenellated parapet) [T], with loopholes. Small pointed turret roofs flank the gate passage [E].
  - A brick bridge crosses the dry grassy moat to the Nowomiejska gate [E].
  - The brick is dark red, about **#8F412F** [E], with mortar about #BDB3A3 [E]. The roofs on the turrets are red tile [E].
- **Walls:**
  - About **1200 m** in total [T]. There is a double line: the **outer, lower wall stands 9–14 m outside the inner one** [T].
  - The outer wall is about **8.5 m** high above the moat floor. The moat is about **4 m** deep [T]. The inner line is about 4 m higher than the outer crest [T].
  - Brick as on the Barbakan. There are many open-backed half-towers [E].
- **Towers:**
  - Baszta Rycerska and Wieża Marszałkowska, the tallest [T].
  - **Baszta Prochowa**, on the Vistula side by Mostowa, rebuilt [T list, E location].
  - Brama Krakowska and Wieża Grodzka (castle) [T].
- **Statue:** the Mały Powstaniec statue stands on the inner wall side, Podwale [T].
- **Sources:**
  - https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barbakan_w_Warszawie
  - https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mury_obronne_w_Warszawie
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw_Barbican

### Kościół św. Anny (Krakowskie Przedmieście 68)
- **Facade:** classicist, designed by Piotr Aigner (1778–88) [T]. A portico with columns and a triangular pediment, plus statues [E]. It is painted a "stone colour", **light cream/stone**, about **#E6DCC6** [T 1846 description, E hex].
- **Bell tower:**
  - A **separate brick campanile** south-west of the facade. It dates from 1578 and was remodelled by Aigner in 1819–21 as an **Italian-Renaissance campanile crowned by a glorieta** [T].
  - It has a **public viewing terrace** [T]. The height is not published in what I found; take it from LiDAR. It is roughly 35–40 m [E].
  - It is plastered in the same cream as the facade [E].
- **Odwach:** on the other side of the facade stands a two-storey arcaded guardhouse range [T].
- **Roof:** copper [E].
- **Sources:**
  - https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ko%C5%9Bci%C3%B3%C5%82_%C5%9Bw._Anny_w_Warszawie_(%C5%9Ar%C3%B3dmie%C5%9Bcie)

## New Town (Nowe Miasto)

### Rynek Nowego Miasta and Kościół św. Kazimierza (sakramentki)
- **The square:**
  - Originally 140 × 120 m [T]. It is now a trapezoid sloping toward the east [T: "zróżnicowanie poziomów"].
  - In the south-west corner stands a **cast-iron well** (19th c.) topped with the New Town arms, a maiden with a unicorn [T]. It is dark green/black iron [E].
  - The houses are 2–4 storeys in pastel plaster [E]. Many south and north houses are loose classicist reconstructions [T].
- **Kościół św. Kazimierza** (Rynek Nowego Miasta 2, east side):
  - Designed by Tylman van Gameren, 1688–92 [T]. It is a Greek-cross church [E]. A **high dome on a drum, with a lantern topped by a royal crown and a cross** [T].
  - The walls are **white/off-white plaster**, about #F0ECE2 [E]. The dome is copper, dark grey-black/grey-green [E, check photos].
  - In front of it, on the square, stands a small baroque convent "palace" (Antonio Solari, about 1745) [T].

### Kościół Nawiedzenia NMP (Przyrynek 2)
- **The church:**
  - Gothic, **red brick**, 1411. It stands **on the escarpment edge** above Podzamcze [T].
  - It is squat, with a **stepped gable**, slender **blind niches**, a pointed portal, and low wide aisles with buttresses [T].
  - The brick is about **#93432F** [E]; the niche plaster is white [E].
  - There are **two white baroque square chapels with green helmets** [T].
- **Bell tower:** a separate square brick tower south of the church, with a tall pitched/pyramidal roof [E]. The height is not published in what I found; take it from LiDAR, roughly 30 m [E].
- **Surroundings:** a square with **tall horse chestnuts** (the former cemetery) [T] and the Łukasiński monument [T].
- **Sources:**
  - https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ko%C5%9Bci%C3%B3%C5%82_Nawiedzenia_Naj%C5%9Bwi%C4%99tszej_Marii_Panny_w_Warszawie

### Kościół św. Jacka (Dominicans, Freta 8/10)
- Built 1603–39, early baroque with post-Gothic elements [T]. The facade on Freta stands above a flight of steps [E].
- Plastered light cream [E]. On the courtyard/escarpment side there is a domed chapel (the Kotowski chapel) [E]. The roofs are red tile or copper; check photos [E].
- **Sources:** https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ko%C5%9Bci%C3%B3%C5%82_%C5%9Bw._Jacka_w_Warszawie

### Multimedialny Park Fontann (Podzamcze)
- **Layout:** four fountains: **three water basins plus one "dry" fountain** (jets from the paving) [T]. Total water surface **2870 m²** [T]. Opened 2011 [T].
- **Location:** bounded by Boleść, Wybrzeże Gdańskie, Sanguszki and Rybaki [T]. It lies at the foot of the escarpment, below the Nawiedzenia church.
- **Surroundings:** the Podzamcze park (12.9 ha, 1971) stretches from Góra Gnojna to Sanguszki [T]. It is mostly lawn and trees [E].
- **Colours:** the basins are shallow, with dark granite/stone rims [E] and light paving [E].
- **Sources:** https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multimedialny_Park_Fontann_w_Warszawie
- **Commons:** `Multimedia Fountain Park at Podzamcze (Warsaw)` (22)

## West edge: Długa, Plac Krasińskich, Plac Bankowy, Muranów

### Pałac Krasińskich (Plac Krasińskich 3/5)
- **Size:** **75.5 × 27.5 m, 19 m high** [T]. A **low hip roof in sheet metal** with **six regular chimneys** [T].
- **Facade:**
  - Shallow end risalits and a central risalit with a **pediment whose relief is by Andreas Schlüter** [T].
  - The **figure of Marcus Valerius** stands on the apex [T].
  - The walls are pale stone/cream, about #E7DCC0 [E]. The sculpture is white/light stone [E]. The roof is copper, greenish [E].
- **Garden:** the Ogród Krasińskich lies behind, to the north [T].
- **Sources:** https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pa%C5%82ac_Krasi%C5%84skich_w_Warszawie
- **Commons:** `Krasiński Palace` (14), `West facade of Krasiński Palace` (86), `East facade of Krasiński Palace` (54) and `Krasiński Garden` (96)

### Pomnik Powstania Warszawskiego (Plac Krasińskich)
- By Wincenty Kućma, unveiled 1 Aug 1989 [T]. **Bronze**, dark brown-green, about #4A4638 [E].
- **Two groups:**
  - A large group of insurgents running from under **tilting slabs of a collapsing wall**, about **10 m** high [T Wikidata P2048].
  - A smaller group descending into a **sewer manhole** [T].
- **Base:** granite slabs / steps [E].
- **Backdrop:** the Supreme Court building (1999) [T]. It has **green glass and green-patinated columns** [E]. It stands out next to the monument and is worth modelling as such.
- **Sources:** https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pomnik_Powstania_Warszawskiego
- **Commons:** `Warsaw Uprising Monument` (150)

### Katedra Polowa Wojska Polskiego (Długa 13/15)
- Baroque, consecrated 1701 [T]. From 1835 it was an Orthodox cathedral with kokoshnik-topped towers [T]. The baroque look was restored in 1923–27 (Sosnowski) [T].
- The facade has **two towers** [T] with baroque caps [E]. It is plastered light (cream/white), about #EEE6D4 [E], with copper roofs [E].
- The Katyń chapel is attached [T].
- **Sources:** https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katedra_polowa_Wojska_Polskiego
- **Commons:** `Church of Our Lady Queen of the Polish Crown in Warsaw` (82). `Field Cathedral of the Polish Army` does not exist.

### Arsenał (Długa 52)
- An early-baroque **closed quadrangle of 64 × 83 m** with a large courtyard [T].
- An **arcade (podcienia) with a pavement** runs along the Nalewki side (1938), with a sgraffito of Władysław IV and Piłsudski [T]. It houses the Archaeological Museum [T].
- Plastered; the colour should come from photos, I recall pale ochre/salmon [E].
- **Sources:** https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arsena%C5%82_w_Warszawie
- **Commons:** `Arsenał in Warsaw` (27)

- The walls are pale ochre/cream [E].

### Błękitny Wieżowiec (Plac Bankowy 2)
- **Height:** **120 m including antenna masts, 27 storeys above ground** [T].

### Pałac Jabłonowskich and the city hall
- **Location:** the Jabłonowski Palace is on **Plac Teatralny / Senatorska 14/16**, NOT on Plac Bankowy [T].
  - It was the city hall in 1819–1944 [T]. It was rebuilt in 1995–97 and is now a bank [T].
  - It has a **tower with clocks on three sides**, the former fire watch-tower [T], and a roof with dormers [T].
  - The walls are light cream/ochre [E]. The roofs are dark (copper or zinc) [E].
- **Current city hall:** at **Plac Bankowy 3/5**, in Corazzi's former Treasury/Commission palaces (1820s). They are classicist, **yellow/ochre plaster with white columns** [E]. These are the long ochre range on the west side of Plac Bankowy.
- **Sources:**

### POLIN museum (Anielewicza 6)
- By Lahdelma & Mahlamäki, opened in 2013/14 [T]. **Height 21 m** [T]. It is a near-square box [E].
- **Exterior:** **glass fins and copper mesh** [T]. "Polin" is silk-screened on the glass in Latin and Hebrew letters [T].
  - From a distance it reads as warm grey-bronze/copper-grey, about **#9A8D7A** [E].
- **The "canyon":** the main hall is a tall wavy canyon cutting the building east–west [T]. It shows on the facade as a big irregular glazed opening, on the side facing the Ghetto Heroes Monument [E].
- **Ghetto Heroes Monument:** dark labradorite with bronze relief, about 11 m [E]. It stands opposite the museum on the square.

## River and Praga

### Most Śląsko-Dąbrowski
- **History:** built 1947–49 by Mostostal (designer Jerzy Koziełek) **on the piers of the Kierbedź bridge** [T]. The heavy Kierbedź lattice trusses were **not** rebuilt [T].
- **Structure:**
  - **Six spans** [T: "sześciu przęseł"], so five river piers plus abutments. The middle pier is no. 4 [T].
  - The steel superstructure sits **entirely below the deck**: deep steel girders, with no trusses or arches above the roadway [E: plate/box girders; I could not confirm whether it is a plate girder or a truss].
  - Spans are about 75–80 m and the total is about 500 m [E, the Kierbedź layout; measure from ortho].
- **Piers:** the 19th-century piers are stone-clad [E], with **pointed cutwaters upstream** [E]. Light grey-beige, about #A9A397 [E].
- **Girders:** painted; check photos for colour, likely grey/green-grey, about #6F7A72 [E].
- **Deck:** trams run in the middle and are separated by a yellow line [T]. The 1993 lighting masts are tubular [T].
- **Sources:**
  - https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Most_%C5%9Al%C4%85sko-D%C4%85browski
  - https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silesian%E2%80%93D%C4%85browa_Bridge
- **Commons:** `Śląsko-Dąbrowski Bridge` (105)

### Katedra św. Michała Archanioła i św. Floriana (Floriańska 3, Praga)
- **Style and plan:** neo-Gothic, 1888–1904 (Józef Pius Dziekoński) [T]. **Two tall west towers**, plus three lower towers: a ridge turret and two stair towers by the chancel [T].
- **Tower height:** the towers are **75 m** [T]. They were shortened in 1932–34, blown up in 1944, and rebuilt to full height; the rebuild finished in **1972** [T].
- **Brick:** **red brick**, specially fired by hand by the Granzow works [T]. About **#A2432E** [E], with sparse light stone details [T: "ograniczono kamienny detal na rzecz cegły"].
- **Roofs and spires:** copper, dark green-grey, about #4F5E55 [E, verify].
- **Sources:** https://pl.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bazylika_Katedralna_%C5%9Bw._Micha%C5%82a_Archanio%C5%82a_i_%C5%9Bw._Floriana_M%C4%99czennika_w_Warszawie
- **Commons:** `Saints Michael Archangel and Florian Basilica in Warsaw` (28) and `Exterior of Saints Michael Archangel and Florian Basilica in Warsaw` (46)

### Warsaw ZOO edge and Park Praski
- **Edges:** the ZOO (ul. Ratuszowa 1/3) runs along **Wybrzeże Helskie**, north of al. Solidarności. To the south is Park Praski [T].
- **Riverside edge:** mature trees behind a fence. Few buildings show from the river [E]. Model it as tree canopy, with a fence line along Wybrzeże Helskie.
- **Bear enclosure:** the **bear enclosure** (wybieg niedźwiedzi) sits in Park Praski at the Praga bridgehead, by al. Solidarności. It is a rocky pit with a moat, visible from the street [E, check ortho].
